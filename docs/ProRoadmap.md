# Pro Tier Roadmap

This document captures candidate business models and Pro-tier features for iNegosyo. Use it as a reference when planning the product roadmap.

## Business Model

Target audience: home-based businesses in the Philippines.

Recommended model: **freemium SaaS**.

- **Free tier**: single user, limited number of customers/orders per month, basic dashboard.
- **Pro tier**: monthly or annual subscription, unlocked growth and operations features.
- Keep pricing low-friction for the local market. Suggested range: **₱299–₱999/month** depending on feature set, with local payment options such as GCash, Maya, or Stripe.
- Optional add-ons: extra team members, white-label shareable storefront, SMS packs.

## Pro-Tier Features

### Branding & Identity

- Custom logo, brand colors, and receipt/order slip header.
- Custom domain or shareable branded order status page.
- White-label customer-facing order link (no iNegosyo branding).

### Inventory & Catalog

- Product catalog with stock levels, units, and variants.
- Stock-in/stock-out tracking tied to orders.
- Low-stock alerts and cost-of-goods reporting.

### Users & Permissions (Multi-User)

- Organization/team support with roles: owner, staff, cashier, rider.
- Staff logins with restricted access per role.
- Activity log / audit trail for data changes.

### Payments & Receivables

- Partial payment tracking per order.
- Send GCash / Maya / PayMongo payment links or QR reminders.
- Auto-computed receivables aging report.
- Cash flow summary.

### Orders & Operations

- Order status workflow with SMS / WhatsApp notifications.
- Delivery rider assignment and delivery tracking.
- Bulk actions: mark paid, mark delivered, export.
- Recurring / subscription orders.

### Analytics & Marketing

- Sales dashboard (daily / weekly / monthly).
- Top customers and top products reports.
- Customer group tagging and bulk messaging.
- Export to CSV / PDF.

### Reliability & Scale

- Offline-first PWA with background sync.
- Automated backups.
- API / webhooks for integrations (e.g., Shopify, Facebook / Messenger orders).

## Suggested Pro MVP Order

1. Branding & white-label basics.
2. Inventory / product catalog.
3. Multi-user support with roles and permissions.
4. Advanced payments, receivables, and payment links.
5. Analytics, reporting, and marketing tools.
6. PWA / offline-first reliability.
